                SECURITY KNOWLEDGE

                Cyber Awareness Hub

                Small security habits can prevent major losses.

                🔐

                Strong Passwords

                Use long, unique passwords and avoid reusing the same password across important accounts.

                🛡️

                Two-Factor Authentication

                Enable 2FA wherever possible to add another layer of protection to your accounts.

                🎣

                Phishing

                Check domains carefully and never trust urgent messages asking you to click unfamiliar links.

                🧠

                Social Engineering

                Scammers manipulate emotions such as fear, urgency, curiosity and greed.

                🌐

                Safe Browsing

                Verify website addresses before entering passwords, payment details or personal information.

                💳

                UPI & Payments

                Never share OTPs or UPI PINs. You do not need to enter a PIN to receive money.

                📱

                Social Media

                Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly and verify unexpected requests.

                🦠

                Malware Protection

                Install software from trusted sources and avoid suspicious attachments and cracked applications.

                💻

                Device Security

                Keep your operating system, browser and security software updated.

                🔒

                Privacy

                Think before sharing personal information, documents, passwords or financial details.

                🚨
                IF YOU ARE TARGETED

                Stop. Don't panic.

                Stop communicating with the scammer, preserve evidence, contact your bank or service provider through official channels, and report the incident to the appropriate cybercrime authorities.
