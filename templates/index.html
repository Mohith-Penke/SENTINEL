              CYBER AWARENESS CENTER

              Stay Ahead of Cyber Fraud

              Learn the most important rules for protecting yourself online.

              🔐

              Protect Your OTP

              Never share an OTP with anyone. Banks and legitimate companies do not need your OTP over a phone call.

              💳

              UPI Safety

              Never share your UPI PIN. To receive money, you normally do not need to enter your UPI PIN.

              🎣

              Phishing

              Check domains carefully before entering passwords, banking information or personal details.

              📱

              Social Engineering

              Scammers may create urgency, fear or excitement to make you act without thinking.

              💼

              Job Scams

              Never pay registration or security deposits for suspicious job offers.

              📈

              Investment Scams

              Guaranteed returns and pressure to deposit money are major warning signs.
