    CYBER AWARENESS CENTER

    Stay One Step Ahead

    Learn simple habits that can protect you from common cyber fraud.

    🔐

    Protect Your OTP

    Never share OTPs with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank or a government service.

    💳

    UPI Safety

    You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. A QR code can be used to request payment.

    🌐

    Check Website URLs

    Look carefully at the domain name before entering passwords, card details or banking information.

    📱

    Fake Apps & Links

    Avoid downloading applications from unknown links sent through SMS, WhatsApp or social media.

    💼

    Job Scams

    Genuine employers do not normally demand money for registration, interviews or guaranteed jobs.

    🚨

    Act Quickly

    If you lose money to cyber fraud in India, immediately contact the cyber crime helpline.
